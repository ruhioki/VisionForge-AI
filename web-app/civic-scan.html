Scan paperwork. Spend less time retyping.

CivicScan Desk is a prototype for public-service counters and community organizations. Turn a clear document photo into editable text, read supported QR/barcodes, and use a checklist to catch missing items before a human review.

1. Capture or choose a document

Choose a document photo or open the camera. The document is processed in this browser.
Privacy: image processing and OCR run on your device. Do not use this demo for highly sensitive records on shared devices. Nothing is submitted to a government database.

2. Extracted text

Review and correct OCR results before copying them into an official system.

QR/barcode reading depends on browser support and whether a code is visible in the photo. This does not verify whether a document is genuine.

3. Application completeness checklist

Use this checklist as a reminder only. Requirements differ by service and location.
